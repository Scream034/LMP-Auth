# LMP Auth Extension

A lightweight browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Opera that securely sends your YouTube Music session to the [Lite Music Player (LMP)](https://github.com/Scream034/LMP) desktop app.

## How to Install (Developer Mode)
1. Download and extract the `.zip` release.
2. Open your browser and go to the extensions page ([Chrome](chrome://extensions/) or [Edge](edge://extensions/)).
3. Enable **Developer mode** in the top right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder.
5. Pin the extension to your toolbar!

## Usage
1. Open LMP and click **Sign In** in Settings.
2. Ensure you are signed in on `music.youtube.com` in your browser.
3. Click the LMP Auth extension icon and press **Send to LMP**.
4. The desktop app will automatically log you in!
